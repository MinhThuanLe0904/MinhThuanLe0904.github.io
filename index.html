Ongoing Capstone · COEN / ELEC 490

PillGuard

Smart medication dispensing system

A team capstone exploring scheduled medication dispensing, single-pill pickup, and patient and caregiver interfaces. The proposed architecture combines a Raspberry Pi, an ESP32, sensors, and a shared mechanical dispensing path.

Raspberry PiESP32SensorsMechanical design

My contribution

Designing the dispensing mechanism.

My Phase 1 work focuses on a six-compartment carousel, one shared vacuum pickup head, and an enclosure that aligns loading, pickup, and retrieval.

Select bin→Pick up pill→Verify drop
Design approach and next steps +

The concept uses one pickup head for all six compartments. Planned prototype trials will check carousel alignment, single-pill pickup, transfer, and physical drop confirmation across representative pill shapes. These are design goals, rather than verified performance results.
